## Entry details and deadlines

### Filmhack - 13-15 November

The 48-hour build window runs **Friday 15:00 to Sunday 15:00**. In-person Friday check-in is required between **10:00 and 14:00**, followed by the 14:00 kick-off. No remote option. Teams can form on site.

The organizer currently accepts only four roles: **Cinematographer, Sound / Music, Editor and Actor / Performer**. Other roles are full. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis; no separate final cutoff is stated. AI creators should already know their tools; traditional filmmakers do not need prior AI experience. Apply through the [official event page](https://filmhack.ai/).

### AI Builders × commIT - 20 November

Doors open 09:45. Solo participants and teams of up to four are welcome; basic AI/LLM familiarity is helpful. Bring a laptop and charger. The day includes matching, building, demos, judging and evening networking.

The ticket types have different minimums and approval conditions. Listed amounts are ticket minimums, not a final checkout quote. The organizer offers a sponsored-ticket/volunteer route by request, not automatic free entry. No application cutoff is stated. Use the [organizer's ticket page](https://lu.ma/ber-nov20-hday).

### HackLab - 21-22 November

Saturday doors open 09:00; hacking starts 11:00 and continues overnight. Sunday project submission closes **17:45**, followed by judging at 18:00 and pitches/awards from 19:00. This is the project deadline, not an application deadline.

For builders, engineers, founders and researchers interested in robotics and AI. The organizer says to complete its [mandatory selection form](https://tally.so/r/vXGxx8) as well as Luma registration, and wait for email acceptance. No application cutoff is stated on the checked event page. [Event and registration](https://luma.com/h0mcaqio).

### Big Berlin Hack - 28-29 November

The listing runs **Saturday 09:30 to Sunday 19:00**; its written Saturday agenda says doors open at 10:00. Confirm the final arrival time with the host. Sunday project submission closes **14:00**, pitches start 15:30 and awards 16:30.

Applications have Founder, Engineer, Researcher / Student, and Product / Design / Commercial categories, each requiring approval. No application cutoff is stated. The event page names The Delta Campus and CODE. Venue address confirmed and matched to Google Maps: [Donaustraße 44, 12043 Berlin-Neukölln](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Delta&query_place_id=ChIJEQ2ZrudPqEcRX0e79wemgGQ). [Apply with the organizer](https://lu.ma/bbh).

## 42 Berlin Eventbrite coverage and entry details

Checked the [42 Berlin Eventbrite organizer](https://www.eventbrite.de/o/42-berlin-60108695013), individual Eventbrite listings and [42 Berlin's own calendar](https://www.42berlin.de/events) on 12 November. The Volkswagen / Databricks event is the only in-window 42 Berlin hackathon found. The older Building Autonomous Coding Agents / Needle listing has ended and is outside this four-week window. Workshops, open days and January events are not hackathons in this view.

[42 × Volkswagen × Databricks Data & AI Hackathon](https://www.eventbrite.de/e/42-x-volkswagen-x-databricks-data-ai-hackathon-the-final-pitches-tickets-2003955497839) runs **19-20 November** for student teams from 42 Berlin and 42 Wolfsburg, working on Volkswagen logistics data. The public listing does **not** establish an open competitor application route or its eligibility rules; ask 42 directly about participation.

The separately advertised **Final Pitches** are open to audience registration on **Fri 20 Nov, 13:30-18:00**, at **[42 Berlin](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=42+Berlin&query_place_id=ChIJteHVQlFPqEcRpDe7mhB93bA), Harzer Straße 39 (organizer address; Google Maps lists Harzer Straße 42)**. Pitches 14:00-16:00, winner announcement/networking 16:30-18:00. This ticket is to watch, not to compete. A ticket price is not visible in the checked event description; confirm on registration.

For technical talks, demos and community evenings, see the separate [Berlin AI Builder Meetups directory](https://github.com/noah-42/berlin-ai-builder-meetups).
